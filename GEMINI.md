# Worthbase

The `worthbase` tools read and change the signed-in person's own household balance sheet on
[Worthbase](https://worthbase.app): what they and their family, trusts or companies own and owe,
its value over time, gains, returns (XIRR), loans and goals. Writes are previewed with `record` and
saved only with `commit` after the person approves; `undo` reverses a batch. Quote the figures the
tools return rather than calculating them. The server's own instructions describe how to use them.

Worthbase has no bank or broker logins, never moves money, doesn't calculate tax and isn't
financial advice.
